📐 Core Identities

1
sin²θ + cos²θ = 1
2
1 + tan²θ = sec²θ
3

🔁 Compound Angle Formulas

1
sin(A ± B) = sinA cosB ± cosA sinB
2
cos(A ± B) = cosA cosB ∓ sinA sinB
3
tan(A ± B) = (tanA ± tanB) / (1 ∓ tanA tanB)

2θ, 3θ & Half-Angle

1
sin 2θ = 2 sinθ cosθ
2
cos 2θ = cos²θ − sin²θ = 2cos²θ − 1 = 1 − 2sin²θ
3
tan 2θ = 2tanθ / (1 − tan²θ)
4
t = tan(θ/2) → sinθ = 2t/(1+t²), cosθ = (1−t²)/(1+t²)

3θ & Product / Sum

1
sin 3θ = 3sinθ − 4sin³θ
2
cos 3θ = 4cos³θ − 3cosθ
3
tan 3θ = (3tanθ − tan³θ) / (1 − 3tan²θ)
4
2sinA cosB = sin(A+B) + sin(A−B)

🧭 General Solutions

1
sinθ = sinα ⇒ θ = nπ + (−1)ⁿα
2
cosθ = cosα ⇒ θ = 2nπ ± α
3
tanθ = tanα ⇒ θ = nπ + α

📌 Sine & Cosine Rules

1
Sine Rule: a/sinA = b/sinB = c/sinC = 2R
2
Cosine Rule: a² = b² + c² − 2bc cosA
3
Area of triangle = ½bc sinA

🔺 Special Triangle Ratios

1
45°–45°–90° triangle → side ratio 1 : 1 : √2
2
30°–60°–90° triangle → side ratio 1 : √3 : 2
3
Heron’s formula: Area = √[s(s−a)(s−b)(s−c)]
